The restaurant OPTIPLAT is moving. It’s time to fill the boxes. Let N be the number of items to pack. The items are numbered from 1 to N. Let vn be the volume of item n. The restaurant has boxes, all with a volume capacity equal to C. The goal is to pack the items into the various boxes so that : (1)the total volume of the items in each box does not exceed the capacity C(ConstraintC1), (2) all items are packed (Constraint C2), (3) the number of used boxes is minimized (Objective O1). This is a problem known as « Bin packing problem ».
